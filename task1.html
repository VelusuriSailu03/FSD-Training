Aditya University

The College is situated in an eco-friendly area of 180 acres with thick greenery at Surampalem, Gandepalli Mandal, East Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh. The College is 15 KM away from Samalkot Railway Station on the Howrah-Chennai Railway line in South Central Railway. The College is 35 KM away from Kakinada and Rajahmundry on ADB Road.

The College has four academic buildings with a total carpet area of 44,524 Sq. Mts. apart from two boys' hostels and one girls' hostel. The departments are equipped with modern laboratories, smart classrooms, seminar halls, and a well-stocked library.

The dreams of its founding members took shape in 2004 in the form of Sri Sai Aditya Institute of Science and Technology, which is now renamed as Aditya College of Engineering and Technology (ACET). It is approved by AICTE and permanently affiliated with JNTU Kakinada.

The campus is centrally located between Kakinada and Rajahmundry. ACET offers undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Engineering, Science, and Management. The college is known for its excellent academic performance, modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, and placement opportunities. It encourages innovation, research, personality development, and extracurricular activities to shape students into successful professionals.
